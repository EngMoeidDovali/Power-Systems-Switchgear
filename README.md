## Standards & Compliance
All designs in this repository adhere to international and national electrical standards to ensure safety, reliability, and interoperability:

- **IEC 61439:** Standard for Low-voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies.
- **IEC 60529:** Degrees of protection provided by enclosures (IP Code).
- **IEC 60204-1:** Safety of machinery – Electrical equipment of machines.
- **Local/National Standards:** Design complies with relevant national electrical codes for industrial installations.

## Design Highlights
- **Enclosure Design:** Optimized for thermal management and environmental protection.
- **Safety Features:** Integration of proper grounding, interlocking systems, and protection relays.
- **Layout:** Logical arrangement for easy maintenance and expansion.
